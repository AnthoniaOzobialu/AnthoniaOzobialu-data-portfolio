# Road Accidents in Nigeria — Data Analysis Report

## Overview
Analysis of road accident trends in Nigeria (2020 Q4–2024 Q1) using 
National Bureau of Statistics data, covering accident cases, deaths, 
injuries, causes, and vehicle types across all 36 states + FCT.

## Objective
Identify the states, vehicle types, and causative factors driving 
Nigeria's road accident rate to inform data-backed safety policy.

## Dataset
- Source: National Bureau of Statistics (NBS)
- Period: 2020 Q4 – 2024 Q1
- Fields: state, cause, vehicle type, casualty severity, victim demographics

## Tools
Power Query · Python (pandas, matplotlib/seaborn) · Power BI · PowerPoint

## Key Findings
- 2022 recorded the highest cases, deaths, and injuries, ahead of 2021 and 2023
- FCT, Kaduna, Ogun, Nasarawa, and Niger are the top 5 states by cases and deaths
- Speed Violation (SPV) is the leading cause nationwide (2,298 of 2024 Q1 cases)
- Male adults are the most affected group; female children the least
- Commercial vehicles account for 71% of all vehicles involved

## Recommendations
- Strengthen traffic regulation enforcement (speed limits, distracted driving)
- Conduct regular safety audits of high-traffic corridors
- Invest in road infrastructure (signage, lighting, surface maintenance)
- Run targeted public safety campaigns on high-risk behaviors
- Expand road safety education programs in schools/communities
